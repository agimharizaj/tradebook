Section 2

Day panel vs Week panel — same drawer, two scopes

Clicking a calendar day opens the drawer for that day. Clicking a Weekly Breakdown card opens the identical drawer scoped to the week: same stats aggregated Mon–Sun, arrows step week by week, the title reads "Week 2 · Aug 3–9", and the trades list shows every trade that week grouped by day. "Plan followed" and the routine section show per-day chips instead of one selector. Everything else is unchanged, so it's one component (JournalPanel) with a scope: day | week prop.

Behaviour notes: the drawer is viewport-pinned on desktop (calendar stays visible), full-screen sheet on mobile with the back gesture closing it (same history-entry trick as the Sidekick dock). Left/right arrows animate the content, "Today" jumps back. Net PnL, win rate and stats respect the $ / % / R toggle.

Charts — trading day panel, open by default

A collapsible panel on the right of the TradingView embed (open by default, state remembered). It holds the pre-market routine checkboxes, live guardrail readouts and warnings. Checks reset each trading day, not on collapse — collapsing shouldn't wipe your progress mid-morning; the daily reset covers the "fresh each session" intent. If the routine isn't complete by your reminder time, a browser notification fires (permission asked once).

Section 8

Build notes — what changes under the hood

Database (new migrations, RLS on everything)

  • 0014_trade_reviews.sql — one row per trade: plan_followed, strategy_id, confluences text[], management text[], mistakes text[], entry_emotion, exit_emotion, reflection, htf_path / mtf_path / ltf_path (entry-models bucket). A trade counts as "journaled" when a review row has a reflection or any chart.
  • 0015_day_reviews.sql — one row per day: plan_followed (yes/partial/no), day_note, routine_done text[], computed violations jsonb.
  • 0016_user_settings.sql — single-row settings: account size, max trades/day, max daily loss, profit target, windows 1+2, routine items jsonb, reminder time, theme, tutorial-done flag. (Simpler than auth metadata and queryable by the AI context.)
  • Tag catalogs are derived (distinct values from trade_reviews), no extra table.

Also picked up from your screenshots (not in your written list)

  • $ / % / R display toggle on the journal header.
  • Expectancy + R-distribution histogram in Monthly Summary; best/worst trade rows.
  • "Today" pill on the calendar and in the day panel title.
  • Emotion shown on each trade row (entry→exit) once journaled.
  • Live guardrail warnings on Charts (outside-window and max-loss banners).
  • Session (London/NY/Tokyo/Sydney) and duration derived on the trade journal page from trade time.

Deliberately left out (say so if you want them)

  • Share Trade public links — Tradebook is single-user and not being sold; a public share URL adds an auth-bypass surface for no benefit.
  • Emotion popup right after placing a trade — EdgeFlo shows it because trades are placed in-app; yours arrive via MT5 import or manual add. The "Save & Journal" flow covers the same intent.
  • Split-order breakdown — MT5 import stores one row per trade today; splitting is a bigger import change, deferred.
  • "Auto" journaling badge — no broker sync yet (roadmap: MetaApi later).

Sidekick

The AI context (ai-context.ts) gains trade reviews, day reviews, settings guardrails and violation history — so you can ask "what mistakes do I repeat on losing days?" and it answers from tags and emotions.

Flags & emoji ruling needed

Your icon rule bans emoji as UI icons. I'm treating pair flags and emotion labels as data, not icons (they match EdgeFlo and render natively on your Mac/iPhone). Non-FX instruments get a monospace badge (XAU, BTC). If you'd rather keep the rule absolute I'll swap flags for SVG roundels — costs a small sprite set.

Suggested build order (each ships independently)

  • 1 — Settings page + guardrails + routine editor (everything else reads from it)
  • 2 — Journal day/week panel + calendar dots + $/%/R toggle + upgraded summaries
  • 3 — Trade journal page (regular + focus) + voice transcription + Save & Journal + modal close fix
  • 4 — Charts trading-day panel + notifications
  • 5 — Tutorial tour + Sidekick context update
Section 9

Gap analysis — everything else EdgeFlo has that Tradebook doesn't

Full sweep of the screenshots against the current app. Three buckets: covered by this concept, missing and worth adding, and gaps that only make sense with broker sync (already on your "later" roadmap).

Covered by this concept

  • Day/week panel, trade journal (regular + focus), tags, emotions, voice reflection, journaled dots, monthly summary with expectancy + R histogram, weekly breakdown cards, guardrails + violations, pre-market routine + notifications, settings, tutorial, $/%/R toggle, Save & Journal, modal close fix, session/duration on trade pages.

Missing from Tradebook — recommend adding (not in your list)

  • Account balance & equity tracking. EdgeFlo's header shows Balance / Open PnL / Equity everywhere. Tradebook has no concept of a running balance. With account size now in Settings we can derive balance = account size + cumulative net PnL and show it on the Dashboard and Charts panel. Cheap, high value: your % guardrails become exact instead of static.
  • Persistent watchlist panel on Charts. EdgeFlo has a right-hand instrument list with flags for one-click symbol switching. Tradebook only has a dropdown. Your watchlist already exists (usePairs()) so this is a rendering job, and it pairs well with the new trading-day panel.
  • Plan dropdown groups Active / Deleted plans. EdgeFlo keeps journals pointing at deleted strategies. Today a hard-deleted Tradebook strategy would orphan its reviews. Fix: trade_reviews stores strategy_id plus a strategy_name snapshot, and the picker shows a Deleted section when relevant.
  • Chart snapshot → journal. EdgeFlo's camera menu (download / copy image). The TradingView embed exposes a screenshot hook; a "Snap to journal" button could drop the current chart straight into a trade's HTF/MTF/LTF slot or into Sidekick. Nice-to-have, small.
  • Close time on manual trades. Duration needs it; MT5 import has it, the manual add form doesn't. Add an optional close-time field.
  • "This month" reset + fullscreen toggle on the calendar header. Trivial.

Only sensible with broker sync — stays on the "later" roadmap (MetaApi)

  • Live open-positions table under the chart (Exit All, per-position edit/close/delete, live PnL).
  • Placing/closing trades from the chart, and the emotion popup at entry time.
  • Real margin health, live equity, split/partial fills, the "Auto" journaled badge.

Present in EdgeFlo, deliberately skipped

  • Share Trade public links and Tweet image — single-user app, no audience, adds an auth-bypass surface.
